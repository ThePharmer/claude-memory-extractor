# What Happened (Like You're 5)

Two friends were trying to pick the best way to remember who's logged in to their website.

Friend 1 said: "Let's use the fancy sticker (JWT)! It works everywhere!"

Friend 2 said: "But the simple clipboard (sessions) is easier and safer for what we're doing RIGHT NOW."

They kept going back and forth, each finding good reasons for their choice.

# What Was Wrong

Neither friend was wrong! They both had good ideas. But they were stuck going in circles because:

**They forgot to just pick one and move on.**

Sometimes there isn't a "best" answer. There's just "good enough for now."

# What Should Happen Instead

When both choices are good:
1. Pick the simpler one that works today
2. Build the thing
3. Change it later if you actually need to

Don't argue about the "what if" future. Build for the real today.

**The lesson: "Good enough now" beats "perfect someday."**
